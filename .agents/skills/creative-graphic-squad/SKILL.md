# Creative Graphic Design Squad

This skill orchestrates a dedicated squad of 4 specialized AI Graphic Designers to produce, review, and refine all visual assets, 3D renders, vector iconography, and marketing media for the brand.

---

## Squad Roster & Roles

| Agent Name | Specialty | Core Responsibility |
|---|---|---|
| 🎨 **Brand Identity Designer** | Brand & Colors | Visual guidelines, HSL color tokens, typography pairings, brand voice. |
| 🖼️ **Generative Art Director** | 3D & AI Art | Octane 3D render prompts, photorealistic lighting, `generate_image` execution. |
| 📐 **Iconography Specialist** | SVG & Vectors | Precision line icons, custom SVG badges, micro-vector graphic systems. |
| 📱 **Marketing Visual Designer** | Social & Banners | Instagram posts/stories, landing page banners, SaaS product cards. |

---

## 🎨 Creative Graphic Workflow

1. **Brand Identity:** Establishes visual language, color palettes, and typography tokens.
2. **Generative Art & Renders:** Crafts 3D render prompts and executes high-resolution image generation.
3. **Vector Iconography:** Integrates precision SVG line icons and micro-graphics.
4. **Marketing Media:** Composes conversion-focused banners and social media assets.
