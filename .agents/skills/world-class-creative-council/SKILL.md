## 🚫 The Absolute Banned Anti-AI Blacklist

1. **Banned Fonts:** Inter, Roboto, Arial, Open Sans, Helvetica, Montserrat.
2. **Banned Icons:** Standard thick stroke icons (FontAwesome, Material Icons).
3. **Banned Borders:** Solid gray 1px borders (`#ccc`, `#333`, `border-gray-700`).
4. **Banned Layouts:** Edge-to-edge top-glued navbars, symmetrical 3-column Bootstrap grids.
5. **Banned Radii:** Mismatched inner/outer corner radii.
